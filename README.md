# TensorAlgo

TensorAlgo is a trading workflow and decision-support platform designed for traders who want a more structured way to prepare, monitor, and review their trading process.

Rather than acting as a broker or automatically placing trades, TensorAlgo focuses on helping users organize their own trading logic into repeatable workflows.

## What TensorAlgo is built around

Trading decisions often involve more than a single indicator or signal. Traders may consider market structure, predefined scenarios, risk limits, contextual data, and rules developed through experience.

TensorAlgo brings these elements into a structured environment where traders can create and work with trading playbooks, monitor market conditions, and review their decision process over time.

The platform includes tools related to:

* Trading playbooks and rule-based workflows
* Market-context analysis
* Futures and crypto market data
* Risk guardrails
* Trade review and performance analysis
* AI-assisted workflow tools

TensorAlgo does **not** execute trades, connect directly to brokers, manage customer funds, or provide personalized financial advice.
